# 7-Day Reflection Guide — Outline

A printable companion to *You Are Loved — When faith feels complicated* by Joelle Merschman.

Each day uses the same gentle template:

> **Day [N] — [Title]**
>
> *Verse* (one line)
>
> *Reflection* (90–140 words, warm, conversational, faith-centered)
>
> *Prompt* (one short journaling question)
>
> *A small prayer* (1–2 sentences)

Designed to be read in under 5 minutes. Print-ready PDF.

---

## Day 1 — Begin Here
- **Verse:** Psalm 139:1 — *“O Lord, you have searched me and known me.”*
- **Idea:** Permission to come as you are — questions, doubts, and all.
- **Prompt:** What part of your faith feels complicated right now?
- **Prayer:** *God, meet me here. Just as I am.*

## Day 2 — When God Feels Far
- **Verse:** Zephaniah 3:17 — *“He will quiet you by his love.”*
- **Idea:** Presence in the silence. God’s nearness isn’t measured by how loud He feels.
- **Prompt:** When was the last time you felt God close? What was that like?
- **Prayer:** *Even in the quiet, I trust You are here.*

## Day 3 — Belovedness
- **Verse:** 1 John 3:1 — *“See what kind of love the Father has given to us, that we should be called children of God.”*
- **Idea:** Returning to the truth underneath everything: you are loved before you are anything else.
- **Prompt:** What would change today if you really believed you were beloved?
- **Prayer:** *I receive Your love. I let it be the truest thing about me.*

## Day 4 — Holding Doubt Gently
- **Verse:** Mark 9:24 — *“I believe; help my unbelief!”*
- **Idea:** Faith with room for honest questions. Doubt isn’t the opposite of faith.
- **Prompt:** What question have you been afraid to ask God?
- **Prayer:** *I bring my questions to You. I trust You can hold them.*

## Day 5 — Soft Surrender
- **Verse:** Matthew 11:28 — *“Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden.”*
- **Idea:** Letting go of the version of faith you outgrew. You’re not falling away — you’re falling into something truer.
- **Prompt:** What are you ready to lay down today?
- **Prayer:** *I release what was never mine to carry.*

## Day 6 — Quiet Hope
- **Verse:** Lamentations 3:22–23 — *“His mercies are new every morning.”*
- **Idea:** The slow, steady kind of hope that doesn’t need to shout.
- **Prompt:** Where are you noticing small mercies right now?
- **Prayer:** *Thank You for the mercies I almost missed.*

## Day 7 — Carry This With You
- **Verse:** Numbers 6:24–26 — *“The Lord bless you and keep you…”*
- **Idea:** A blessing for the road. What to do next.
- **Prompt:** What one truth do you want to carry forward from this week?
- **Prayer:** *Keep this close to my heart. Keep me close to Yours.*
