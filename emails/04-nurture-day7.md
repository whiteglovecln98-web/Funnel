Hi {{contact.first_name | default: "friend"}},

For a long time, I thought doubt was the enemy of faith.

Then I started reading the Psalms.

David — the man after God’s own heart — spends entire chapters asking *Where are you? Why have you forgotten me? How long, Lord?* And God doesn’t flinch. He doesn’t correct him. He doesn’t love him less for asking.

That changed everything for me.

> *“I believe; help my unbelief!”*
> — Mark 9:24

Doubt isn’t the opposite of faith. **Indifference is.** If you’re still asking the questions, still reaching, still showing up to wrestle — that’s faith. The complicated, honest, deeply-loved kind.

So here’s your gentle reminder this week:
You don’t have to perform certainty to be welcome here. You don’t have to perform certainty to be loved by God.

Your questions are safe.
You are safe.
You are loved.

With love,
Joelle
