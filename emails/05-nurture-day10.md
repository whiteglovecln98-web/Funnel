Hi {{contact.first_name | default: "friend"}},

A lot of people who feel like they’re “losing their faith” aren’t losing their faith at all.

They’re losing a version of it that wasn’t built to hold a real life.

That version — the one with all the right answers, the tidy formulas, the certainty performed for other people — was never the real thing. It was the scaffolding. And sometimes the scaffolding has to come down so the real thing can finally grow.

> *“Come to me, all who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.”*
> — Matthew 11:28

If you’re in a season of letting go, please hear me:
You’re not falling away. You’re falling *into* something truer.

The God who loves you isn’t standing at the edge of your deconstruction with crossed arms. He’s in the middle of it with you, holding the pieces, whispering the same thing He’s been whispering all along:

**You are loved. You are loved. You are loved.**

That’s the heart of everything I write, and it’s the heart of the book.

If these emails have meant something to you, the book is the longer, slower, deeper version of this same conversation. It’s the one I’d hand you in person if I could.

👉 **[Get *You Are Loved* on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0GX2XLSRZ)**

And whether you ever pick up the book or not — please know:
You are still, always, completely loved.

With so much love,
Joelle
