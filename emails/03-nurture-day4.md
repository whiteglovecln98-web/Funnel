Hi {{contact.first_name | default: "friend"}},

Can I tell you something a little vulnerable?

There was a season in my life where I would pray and feel like the words just hit the ceiling and slid back down. I wasn’t in a faith crisis exactly — I just felt the absence of something I used to feel.

If you’ve ever been there, this is for you.

> *“The Lord your God is in your midst, a mighty one who will save; he will rejoice over you with gladness; he will quiet you by his love.”*
> — Zephaniah 3:17

What I’ve come to believe — slowly, and through a lot of complicated seasons — is that God’s nearness isn’t measured by how loud He feels. Sometimes love is quiet on purpose. Sometimes the silence isn’t absence; it’s closeness so steady you stopped noticing it.

If today feels like one of those quiet days, here’s the only thing I want you to take with you:

**You are not less loved when you feel less.**

That’s it. That’s the whole sermon. 💛
